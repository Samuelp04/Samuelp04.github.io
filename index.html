TechFix Pro Backups

TechFix Pro Backups saves backup copies of TechFix Pro — the software a small computer repair business uses for its repair tickets, customers and invoices — to the business owner's own Google Drive.

It only creates and manages its own backup files in a "TechFix Pro Backups" folder. It can't see or change anything else in the Google Drive. It is a private tool used by the business itself and isn't offered to the public.
