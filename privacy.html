Privacy policy – TechFix Pro Backups

TechFix Pro Backups is a private tool a small computer repair business uses to back up its own TechFix Pro software to its own Google Drive.

What it accesses in Google

  • Google Drive, its own files only (the drive.file permission). It creates a "TechFix Pro Backups" folder, uploads backup files into it, lists them, downloads one when a backup is restored, and moves old backups to the Drive trash. It can't see or change any other file in the Drive.
  • The Google account's email address and name, only to show which account the backups go to.

How the data is used, stored and shared

  • The access Google grants is saved only on the business's own computers, in TechFix Pro's settings file. It is used only to save and restore backups.
  • Nothing from Google is sent to anyone else, sold, or used for advertising.

TechFix Pro Backups' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. Backups can be deleted from the "TechFix Pro Backups" folder in Google Drive.
